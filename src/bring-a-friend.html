Bring-a-Friend Cards

Two cards per page — cut along the dashed line and hand one to anyone who has ever said “I always wanted to learn chess.”

Bring a Friend
to Chess Night

A friendly pop-up chess table. Beginners, lapsed players, and curious onlookers all welcome.

  • Never played? You start with one tiny game — pawns only — and one new idea per game after that. Eight tiny games to real chess.
  • No pieces, no chess clock, no rating needed. We bring the boards. You bring yourself and optionally snacks.
  • No signups, no signup sheets. Walk in, sit down, play pawns. Bring this card and your first rematch is reserved.
