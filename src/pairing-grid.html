How to pair by hand (Swiss, the easy way): (1) sort everyone by points so far; (2) split each score group in half — top half plays bottom half; (3) check the grid so nobody plays the same person twice; (4) left over with an odd number? lowest score sits out and collects a ½-point bye; (5) before round 1, pair newcomers with newcomers.

After the last round: highest total on the tally sheet takes the night. Tied? Head-to-head result wins it; still tied, call it co-champions and give stamps to both. Nobody is ranked, nobody is deducted, and beginners may always be paired with beginners instead.
