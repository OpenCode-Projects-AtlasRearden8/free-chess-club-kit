Scoring, the friendly version: write 1 for a win, ½ for a draw, 0 for a loss — or use ticks and add up at the end. A sitting-out player earns a ½-point bye per round. Announce the winner when everyone’s done, not before; the last round can change the whole board.

End-of-night ritual: hand out Progress Passport stamps, copy the venue’s result column onto anything that must survive, wipe the boards, and put the tally sheet in the club folder. Beginners may play ladder games instead — mark their row with an (L) so the pairing grid sees them.

Print free • pencil preferred, eraser expected • the tally sheet documents the night, never judges it
